Builds and trains the autoencoder, a small neural network that compresses the 36 weekly features for a province into a 10 number summary, called a latent state, then tries to reconstruct the original 36 numbers from that summary. Before training the final version, it runs a 100 trial Bayesian search that jointly tries different summary sizes, 3 to 10 numbers, together with the network's other settings, all at once, to find the best combination. A 10 number summary won. It then trains the final network on the training and validation years and checks how well it reconstructs the original features on the sealed 2022-2023 test years.
