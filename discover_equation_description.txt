Runs a search over 72 combinations of three settings that shape how the equation discovery step behaves, tau, a knob controlling how strongly the search favors a simpler equation over a closer fit, the scoring method, one of four standard statistical fitting techniques used to score each candidate equation, and how spread out the starting guesses are across the parallel search groups, wide or narrow. Each of the 72 combinations gets its own full run of the search plus one language model suggestion round, all 72 are ranked by how well they do on validation data, and the best one is then carried into the main pipeline script for the final, one time test.
